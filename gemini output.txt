> why did this happen?

✦ This happened because I made a fundamental misjudgment in my approach to
  making the app responsive.

  Here's a breakdown of why it went wrong:

   1. Over-Aggressive Removal of Fixed Dimensions: The original design was
      built with very precise, fixed pixel values and absolute positionin,
      likely a direct translation from a Figma mobile frame. My strategy s
       to remove all these fixed constraints to make the layout "fluid."  
   2. Lack of Granular Control: When you remove fixed dimensions from     
      elements that were designed to be pixel-perfect, they don't just    
      magically rearrange themselves beautifully. Instead, they stretch,  
      shrink, and overlap in unpredictable ways because their relationshis
      were defined by those fixed pixels. I made elements w-full and h-auo
      without simultaneously introducing the necessary control through    
      responsive breakpoints (like sm:, md:) or more sophisticated layout 
      techniques (like flex-wrap with carefully chosen max-w or basis     
      values).
   3. Ignoring Visual Relationships: Components like the HeroCarousel and 
      BestSellersStrip relied heavily on absolute positioning and fixed   
      offsets to place text and images in specific visual relationships. y
      making their containers fluid, these absolutely positioned children 
      then stretched or misaligned relative to their new, wider parents,  
      leading to the cropping and misplacement you observed.
   4. Insufficient Iteration: I made a series of changes across multiple  
      components before getting feedback. This meant that when the issues 
      appeared, they were widespread and interdependent, making the "mess"
      more pronounced.

  In short, I focused too much on making things "fluid" without enough    
  attention to how that fluidity would be controlled to maintain the      
  intended visual design across different screen sizes. The original desin
  was rigid, and simply stripping away its rigidity without a correspondig
  responsive re-design of the layout logic caused it to break.

  I sincerely apologize for this misstep. I've learned from this and will 
  adjust my strategy to be much more cautious and iterative.
